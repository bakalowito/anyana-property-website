THE DEVELOPER

Built by Antel Land.
Trusted for Nearly 40 Years.

Anyana is a master-planned township by Antel Land, one of the Philippines' most established real estate developers. Founded in 1986, Antel began with residential and corporate high-rise buildings before expanding into large-scale horizontal townships across Cavite and Metro Manila — building communities designed to last for generations.

🏗️
Established 1986Nearly 40 years of experience in Philippine real estate development, from high-rise buildings to horizontal townships.

STRATEGIC ACCESS

Close to the Roads That Matter

Anyana sits within reach of Cavite's growing expressway network, keeping you connected to Metro Manila and beyond.

🛣️

CAVITEX

The Manila-Cavite Expressway offers a direct route toward Metro Manila, easing the daily commute for residents.

🚧

CALAX Interchange

The Cavite-Laguna Expressway links Cavite to Laguna and the greater CALABARZON region, connecting through to CAVITEX for wider access across the south.
